Research Opportunities

We are actively recruiting PhD students and research assistants to join our team at HKU. We welcome motivated individuals passionate about AI, building science, urban resilience, and social equity.

PhD Position 1–2 Openings · 2027 Spring/Fall

PhD Studentship in Urban Building Energy, AI & Socio-Physical Modeling

We are recruiting 1–2 PhD students (intake: 2027 Spring or Fall) to work on research at the intersection of urban microclimate modeling, AI-driven energy forecasting, and socio-demographic behavior simulation.

Research Areas

  • Urban microclimate modeling and localized energy demand forecasting
  • Physics-informed machine learning and graph neural networks
  • Reinforcement learning for human behavior and energy consumption simulation
  • Infrastructure optimization under physical and socioeconomic constraints

Requirements

  • Master's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Urban Science, Social Sciences, or related fields
  • Strong programming skills; experience in deep learning or energy simulation at various scales is a plus
  • Interest in urban energy systems, climate adaptation, or computational social science
  • Strong initiative and excellent English communication skills

About PhD Studies at HKU

The University of Hong Kong (HKU) is a leading research university in Asia. PhD students receive competitive scholarships and have access to world-class research facilities.

PhD students may be supported by the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS), the HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme (HKU-PS), or the HKU Postgraduate Scholarship (PGS), the general funding scheme available to all admitted PhD students.
